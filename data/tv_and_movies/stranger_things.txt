Title: Stranger Things

Stranger Things is a science fiction horror television show created by the Duffer Brothers and released on Netflix.

The show follows a group of children as they navigate supernatural events involving monsters, the Upside Down, and mysterious experiments.

Main characters:
- Eleven (El): A child with supernatural abilities.
- Will Byers: A child connected to the Upside Down.
- Mike Wheeler: One of Eleven's closest friends.
- Lucas Sinclair: A logical and realistic member of the group.
- Dustin Henderson: The intelligent and humorous member of the group.
- Max Mayfield: An adventurous member of the group.

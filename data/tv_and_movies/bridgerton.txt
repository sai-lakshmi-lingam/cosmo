Bridgerton is a period tv show that follows the lives of the Bridgerton family. 
The family consists of 8 children and matriarch, Violet. 
The 8 children include: 
- Anthony: Viscount, oldest of the 8, married to Kate Sharma (Season 2)
- Benedict: Second oldest, lover of the arts, married to Sophie Park (Season 4)
- Colin: traveler, married to Penelope Featherington (Season 3)
- Daphne: eldest daughter, married to Simon, Duke of Hastings (Season 1)
- Eloise: bookworm, not yet married, defiant of the roles women take in Society
- Francesca: quiet piano player, recently became a widow after her husband, John died (married season 3, husband died Season 4)
- Gregory: youngest boy
- Hyacinth: youngest girl

The show follows this family as they navigate love, drama, and a gossip blog that goes by Lady Whistledown.

A mockumentary-style show that follows a group of teachers at an underfunded elementary school. 
The main cast includes: 
- Janine Teagues, hyper positive teacher, 2nd grade
- Mrs. Melissa Schemmenti, questionable tactics and tough love, teaches 2nd grade
- Mrs. Barbara Howard, God-fearing woman, teaches Kindergarten
- Gregory Eddie, worrywart and not adventurous with food, teaches 3rd grade
- Jacob Hill, progressive history teacher, 8th grade
- Mr.Johnson, first name unknown, janitor

Clean Girl makeup is a trend from the early 2020's, where makeup is worn mostly to 
enhance natural features. These looks often had similar concepts in mind: 
- accentuated blush
- moderate usage of coverage products
- naturally colored lip shades
- minimal eyeshadow and eyeliner

These trends were put into affect due to celebrities like Hailey Bieber and others following suit.

While this makeup trend has become less popular within 2026, it's still common to see.

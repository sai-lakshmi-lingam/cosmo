Full Glam makeup is a trend from the late 2010's, where makeup was worn mostly to express creatuvity. 
These looks often had similar concepts in mind: 
- more focus on contour and bronzing products
- heavy coverage products
- brightly colored lipstick and lip gloss
- colorful eyeshadow and eyeliner

These trends were created mostly by beauty YouTubers, who encouraged experimenting with makeup as a form of self expression.
2026 has seen a resurgence of this trend.

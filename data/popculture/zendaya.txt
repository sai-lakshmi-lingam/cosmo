Zendaya is a singer-dancer-actor, most known for starring in the Disney Channel shows Shake it Up and K.C. Undercover. 
After her work on Disney Channel, she has acted in multiple popular projects, including the new installment of 
Spider Man movies, where she works alongside Tom Holland. 

Zendaya is known for other projects, including: 
- The Drama, with Robert Pattinson
- Dune, with Timothee Chalamet
- Euphoria, with Hunter Shafer and Jacob Elordi
- Challengers
- The Greatest Showman, with Zac Efron and Hugh Jackman

Zendaya is also seen as a fashion icon, often stunning audiences with her movie and show premiere clothing. 
Zendaya is married to Tom Holland as of July 2026.

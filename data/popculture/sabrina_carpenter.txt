Sabrina Carpenter is an American pop singer, known for her recent hits: 
- Manchild
- Nonsense
- Please Please Please
- Tears
- Feather
- Taste
- Espresso

She also spent a few years playing Maya on the Disney Channel show Girl Meets World. 
Her style consists of babydoll dresses and other inspired clothing, frequently referencing
past Hollywood looks for red carpets and special events.

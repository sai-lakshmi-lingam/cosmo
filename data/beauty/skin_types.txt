Generally, there are three types of skin, including oily, dry and combination. 

Oily skin tends to have the following characteristics: 
- shiny complexion in the t-zone
- acne susceptable
- look of enlarged pores

Combination skin has the following characteristics: 
- enlarged pores and oily t-zone
- dry or normal cheeks

Dry skin has the following characteristics: 
- roughness or flakiness
- redness
- tightness after washing face
